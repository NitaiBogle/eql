Pick Your Blood — Race Selection Guide

EQL only race-locks your primary class — the other two are free picks you can swap in any city. The question isn’t "what race is my build," it’s "which class do I lock to capture the best racial?"

Only Your Primary Is Locked

Your race gates one class — the primary. Classes two and three are free and swappable in any city at any time. Spend the race lock on whichever class benefits most from your bloodline’s racial abilities.

Two Racials Rule the Meta

Iksar — HP regen + AC scaling (+15→+35) for any melee or pet body. Ogre — frontal stun immunity for tanks facing D4 chain-stun. Everything else is a minor tax break.

Race Is an Opener, Not a Cage

No Ogre? The Steadfast Will AA grants full stun immunity to any race. Froglok now broadly playable (Monk, SK, Shaman, Paladin). Gear is mostly race-unlocked.

Why the Same Five Classes Dominate

If it feels like Cleric, Shaman, Enchanter, Monk, and a pet class appear in almost everything — that’s not lazy theorycrafting. It’s the meta the limitations create. With no mercs and no boxing, every trio privately solves the holy trinity, and four classes each own one corner outright: Cleric owns healing (Promise Renewal), Shaman owns mitigation-through-slow, Enchanter owns crowd control and the highest DPS ceiling (charm), and Monk owns melee damage with a built-in escape (Feign Death). A pet class offers a fifth path — a body that tanks and ignores buff-strip.

The art of building a trio is choosing which engines to double down on. Want fastest clears? Monk + charm. Want to never die? Cleric + Shaman + mez. Want a true tank? Warrior or Paladin up front. The recurring names are the load-bearing walls; the third slot is where your playstyle lives. Shadow Knight sits at the intersection of tank, lifetap sustain, and AoE aggro — a perennial top pick that the lifetap nerf softened but couldn’t dethrone. Necromancer makes a strong case as the "upgrade" over pure melee: DoT pressure, an emergency pet tank, and the single most reliable panic ability in Feign Death.

▴ Corrected — Prior Version Was Factually Wrong

CORRECTED
Charm + Summoned Pet Cannot Coexist
Prior version listed this as an open “Beta Watch” question. Confirmed via official dev statement (Discord, Mar 2026) and Unofficial FAQ: charm counts against the one-active-pet cap. You cannot run a summoned pet and a charmed mob simultaneously — you must choose one. Affected: Build #2 description & pro/con, Build #12 description & pro, the “One Pet” Limitations card, the “Charm Is Conditional” card, the hero note, and the S-Tier compare sidebar for #2.
CORRECTED
SK Soul Abrasion AA Was Not Gutted
Prior version stated “SK’s Soul Abrasion AA gutted” in the Limitations card. Confirmed via beta patch notes: Soul Abrasion is +200% lifetap proc damage — the AA was not nerfed. The nerf hit base lifetap damage/heal scaling only. Affected: “Life Taps Got Nerfed” Limitations card (retitled “Base Only”), Build #3 description, Build #9 con.
CORRECTED
Magician Earth Pet Is Warrior/Ranger
Prior version called the Mag pet “Ranger-class.” Confirmed via EQL Wiki patch notes (June 2, 2026): all summoned pets gain Warrior as a base class plus a secondary. Magician Earth pets are Warrior/Ranger. Enchanter animations are Warrior/Paladin. Beastlord Warders are Warrior/Berserker. Affected: Build #2 description.
CORRECTED
Druid’s New Lines Are Damage-Role, Not a Heal Line
Prior version assumed one of Druid’s two new spell lines was a heal line. Confirmed via Combo Builder patch notes: both new lines are “exclusive to damage roles.” Druid’s healing profile is unchanged; the upgrade is to its DPS ceiling. Affected: Beta Watch “The Hidden Druid Line” card (retitled “Druid DPS Lines Landed”).
CORRECTED
Berserker Stance Provides Double Attack Speed, Not “No Bonus”
Prior version’s Traps entry stated “Berserker Stance provides no hit bonus.” Confirmed: Berserker Stance grants double attack speed (a significant DPS modifier, not an accuracy bonus). The core message — that Monk still out-hits Berserker — remains valid. Affected: Traps card for “Two-Handed Berserker Without a Real Tank.”

Source: EQL Wiki + Combo Builder patch notes through Jun 21, 2026. No tune-down to Promise Renewal found in any closed-beta patch note. The “always bring a Cleric” assumption remains intact entering the July 1 pre-order beta. Beta Watch card updated to reflect survival status.

+ New Builds Added

NEW #4
SK / Shaman / Enchanter “The Charm Reaver” — S-Tier
Community-confirmed S-tier omission from v1.0/v1.1. SK provides the self-sustaining tank body (Soul Abrasion + Shaman slow) while Enchanter provides the highest single-target DPS in the game (unbreakable-charm AA), plus full debuff redundancy (dual slow), mez, runes, and Clarity. The highest burst DPS ceiling of all S-tiers when charm lands alongside the Soul Abrasion proc.
NEW #6
Warrior / Cleric / Enchanter “The Ironclad Charmer” — A-Tier
The only build where the tank side carries zero strippable mana — buff-strip at D4 does literally nothing to the Warrior. Highest D4 raid score in the A-tier. Ogre Warrior stun immunity + Enchanter runes create dual chain-stun counters. Warrior Heroic Leap (AoE taunt, 8 targets) solves the pack problem Enchanter builds otherwise struggle with.
NEW #9
Paladin / Bard / Rogue “The Stun Cascade” — A-Tier
Best stun output in the game — directly counter D4 chain-stun casters by stunning them first (Bard Overhaste multiplies stun proc rate). Rogue beta rework (Chaotic Stab autogranted at level 1, new poison system, Escape ability) finally makes Rogue a top-tier DPS body without positional cheese. Rogue Escape + Paladin Lay on Hands = two independent survival buttons.
